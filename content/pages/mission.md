# Our Mission

We believe families thrive when they have help they can actually trust — and that trust shouldn't be something parents have to guess at.

Primrose Trusted Care exists to do the hard work of vetting providers so families don't have to. Every nanny, sitter, mother's helper, and pet sitter on our roster has been personally interviewed, background-checked, and credential-verified before they ever meet your family.

We're a small team called to serve the families in our community. That sense of calling shapes how we screen, how we match, and how we show up for the people who rely on us.

When you invite a Primrose Trusted Care provider into your home, you're not getting someone we found online — you're getting someone we know.
