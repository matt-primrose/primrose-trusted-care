# Our Mission

At Primrose Trusted Care, we believe children are one of God's greatest blessings, each uniquely created and deeply valued, deserving of care that is safe, attentive, and wholehearted. As a faith-based nanny placement agency, we're called to serve families with the same integrity, compassion, and protection we'd want for our own.

We do the hard work of vetting providers so families don't have to. Every nanny, sitter, mother's helper, and pet sitter on our roster has been personally interviewed, background-checked, and credential-verified before they ever meet your family.

We screen caregivers for more than skill and experience. We look for the same character we'd want in someone who walks through our own front door. That means integrity, patience, and a genuine heart for the children in their care.

When you invite a Primrose Trusted Care provider into your home, you're not getting someone we found online. You're getting someone we know. Someone called, like we are, to care for your family well.
